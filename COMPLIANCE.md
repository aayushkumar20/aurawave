## 2. Content Hosting & Transmission Policy
- **No Media Hosting**: AuraWave does not host, store, cache, distribute, or archive any audio, video, or copyrighted media files on any server owned or operated by the developers.
- **Client-Side Pipeline**: The application functions exclusively as a client-side parser and media player. When an end-user queries a track, the client application resolves publicly available stream metadata on demand. The audio bytes are transferred directly from the content delivery network (CDN) to the end-user's local device hardware decoder.
- **No Re-Broadcasting**: The project developers do not re-encode, restream, or re-transmit any media content through proxy servers.
